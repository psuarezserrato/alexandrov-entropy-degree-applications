For a \(d\)-fold cyclic branched cover \(M_d \to M\) (\(d \ge 2\)), the quotient \(X = M_d / \mathbb {Z}_d\) carries normal cone angle \(\alpha = 2\pi / d \le \pi {\lt} 2\pi \) along the branch locus \(\Sigma \), giving Bishop–Gromov density \(\theta _N(x_0) = \alpha /(2\pi ) {\lt} 1\) and hence \(\mathcal{S}_X \neq \emptyset \).

Let \(M\) be a closed hyperbolic \(N\)-manifold (\(N \ge 3\)) and \(X = M_d / \mathbb {Z}_d\) (\(d \ge 2\)) the orbifold quotient of a branched cover along a nonempty codimension-\(2\) locus \(\Sigma \) carrying a negatively curved metric (\(h(\overline{X}) \le N - 1\)). Then

\[ \mathcal{H}^N(M_d / \mathbb {Z}_d) {\gt} \operatorname {Vol}(M) \qquad \text{and} \qquad \operatorname {Vol}(M_d) {\gt} d \cdot \operatorname {Vol}(M). \]
Proof ▶

Since \(\alpha = 2\pi /d {\lt} 2\pi \) forces \(\mathcal{S}_X \neq \emptyset \), the strictness and matched-entropy lemmas give \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) {\gt} \operatorname {Vol}(M)\), and multiplying by \(d \ge 2\) yields \(\operatorname {Vol}(M_d) = d\, \mathcal{H}^N(X) {\gt} d\, \operatorname {Vol}(M)\).

With Chern–Gauss–Bonnet constant \(C_4 = 4\pi ^2/3 {\gt} 0\), a \(4\)-orbifold \((X, g_E)\) with \(\mathrm{Ric}(g_E) = -3 g_E\) satisfies \(h(\overline{X}) \le 3\) and Satake’s inequality \(\operatorname {Vol}(X, g_E) \le \frac{4\pi ^2}{3}\chi _{\mathrm{orb}}(X)\), while a hyperbolic \(4\)-manifold \(Y\) satisfies \(h(\widetilde{Y}) = 3\) and \(\operatorname {Vol}(Y) = \frac{4\pi ^2}{3}\chi (Y)\).

Under a continuous map \(f : X \to Y\) as above, \(\chi _{\mathrm{orb}}(X) \ge |\deg (f)|\, \chi (Y)\). Moreover, if \(\mathcal{S}_X \neq \emptyset \) and \(|\deg (f)| = [\pi _1(Y):f_*\pi _1(X)] \ge 1\) (forcing \(\deg (f) \neq 0\)), then strict inequality holds: \(\chi _{\mathrm{orb}}(X) {\gt} |\deg (f)|\, \chi (Y)\).

Proof ▶

Cancel \(3^4 = 81\) via matched entropy \(h(\overline{X}) \le 3\) and cancel \(4\pi ^2/3 {\gt} 0\) in the Satake Chern–Gauss–Bonnet inequalities; nonempty \(\mathcal{S}_X\) upgrades \(\le \) to \({\lt}\).

Corollary 2.5 Corollary 1.3(c): Gromov–Thurston Branch-Locus Euler Sign

For \(X = M_d / \mathbb {Z}_d\) (\(d \ge 2\)) branched along a closed surface \(\Sigma \subset M\), any Einstein metric \(\mathrm{Ric}(g_E) = -3 g_E\) on \(X\) forces \(\chi (\Sigma ) {\lt} 0\).

Proof ▶

Combining the orbifold Riemann–Hurwitz identity \(\chi _{\mathrm{orb}}(X) = \chi (M) - (1 - 1/d)\chi (\Sigma )\) with \(\chi _{\mathrm{orb}}(X) {\gt} \chi (M)\) and \(1 - 1/d {\gt} 0\) yields \(\chi (\Sigma ) {\lt} 0\).
