Using Gromov–Thurston proportionality \(\operatorname {Vol}(Y) = v_N \| Y\| \), we have \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) \ge |\deg (f)| v_N \| Y\| \). For a homotopy equivalence, \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) \ge v_N \| X\| \) with equality iff \(X \cong Y\), and \(\mathcal{S}_X \neq \emptyset \) forces strict inequality \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) {\gt} v_N \| X\| \).

Proof ▶

On any closed hyperbolic \(N\)-manifold \((M, g_{\mathrm{hyp}})\) (\(N \ge 3\)), every hyperbolic cone-metric \(g_{\mathrm{cone}}\) with cone angles \(\le 2\pi \) and nonempty singular locus satisfies \(\mathcal{H}^N(M, g_{\mathrm{cone}}) {\gt} \operatorname {Vol}(M, g_{\mathrm{hyp}})\).

Proof ▶

Apply Corollary 1.13 to \(\operatorname {id}_M : (M, g_{\mathrm{cone}}) \to (M, g_{\mathrm{hyp}})\).

Along a torsion-free Selberg finite cover \(p : M \to \mathcal{O}\) with local isotropy orders \(m_i \ge 1\), the lifted cone angle satisfies \(m_i \alpha _i \le 2\pi \iff \alpha _i \le 2\pi / m_i\) (and similarly for \({\lt}\)).

Proof ▶

Multiply or divide both sides by \((m_i : \mathbb {R}) {\gt} 0\).

On a compact orientable negatively curved locally symmetric \(N\)-orbifold (\(\mathcal{O}, g_{\mathrm{sym}}\)) (\(N \ge 3\)), any singular cone-metric \(g_{\mathrm{cone}}\) with \(K_{\mathrm{reg}} \ge -\kappa _0^2\) and cone angles \(\alpha _i \le 2\pi / m_i\) satisfies \(\mathcal{H}^N(|\mathcal{O}|, g_{\mathrm{cone}}) \ge \operatorname {Vol}(\mathcal{O}, g_{\mathrm{sym}})\), with strict inequality whenever some \(\alpha _i {\lt} 2\pi / m_i\).

Proof ▶

Lift to a degree-\(d\) (\(d \ge 1\)) Selberg manifold cover \(M\), apply Theorem 1.1 (or its strictness corollary when \(m_i \alpha _i {\lt} 2\pi \)) to \(\operatorname {id}_M\), and divide by \(d {\gt} 0\).
