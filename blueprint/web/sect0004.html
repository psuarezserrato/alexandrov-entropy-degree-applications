For a relative degree-\(k\) map \(f : (Z, \partial Z) \to (M, \partial M)\) into a compact acylindrical \(3\)-manifold \(M\) with Thurston geodesic-boundary model \(\psi : M \to M_{\mathrm{geod}}\) (\(|\deg (\psi )| = 1\)), Perelman doubling of \(C_Z\) and Storm’s identity \(\operatorname {Vol}(DC_{M_{\mathrm{geod}}}) = \| DM\| \) (or \(v_3 \| DM\| \)) yield \(\operatorname {Vol}(C_Z) \ge \frac{|k|}{2}\, \| DM\| \).

Proof ▶

Use \(|\deg (\psi \circ f)| = |k|\) and matched entropy \(h_0 = 2\) on the doubles, then divide by \(2\).

Theorem 4.3 Theorem 1.10: Boundary Volume Inequality and Rigidity

For a compact orientable \(N\)-dimensional (\(N \ge 3\)) \(\mathrm{Alex}(-1)\) space \(X\) with boundary mapped with degree \(k \neq 0\) to a compact hyperbolic \(N\)-manifold \(Y\) with totally geodesic boundary, \(\operatorname {Vol}(X) \ge |k|\, \operatorname {Vol}(Y)\). Moreover, if \(\operatorname {Vol}(X) = |k|\, \operatorname {Vol}(Y)\) and \(|k| = [\pi _1(DY) : (Df)_* \pi _1(DX)]\), then \(h(\overline{DX}) = N - 1\) (homothety factor \(c = 1\)), \(\mathcal{S}_{DX} = \emptyset \), \(DX\) is globally isometric to the degree-\(|k|\) cover \(\widehat{DY}\), \(\partial X = \mathrm{Fix}(\iota _{DX})\) is totally geodesic, and \(|k| = 1\) implies \(X \cong Y\).

Proof ▶

Volume equality \(\operatorname {Vol}(DX) = |k|\, \operatorname {Vol}(DY) {\gt} 0\) together with Theorem 1.1 sandwiches \((N-1)^N \le h(\overline{DX})^N \le (N-1)^N\), forcing \(h(\overline{DX}) = N - 1\) by strict monotonicity of \(x \mapsto x^N\) on \([0, \infty )\) and triggering unrescaled (\(c = 1\)) rigidity.
